Privacy Policy - One More Throw

Last updated: 2026-11-23

One More Throw ("the app") is a casual paper-plane throwing game published by Hamri (contact: hamri2985@gmail.com). The app is free and shows ads (banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads) provided by Google AdMob and, depending on the app version, by Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads.

Summary

  • Your game data (progress, stars, coins, skins, settings) stays on your device.
  • The app has no accounts and we do not collect personal data ourselves.
  • The advertising SDKs that show the ads (Google Mobile Ads, and Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads where used) collect some device and usage data and share it with their providers.

Data stored on your device

Saved only on your device: level progress, stars and attempts, coins, unlocked and chosen plane skins, hints, the Daily Throw state, and your settings (sound, vibration, reduce motion, video mode, language). This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Advertising partners

Ads in the app are delivered by these partners, whose SDKs are built into the app:

  • Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies, including ironSource): ad mediation that chooses which ad network shows each ad.
  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies): an ad network that serves ads through LevelPlay.
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google): an ad partner, and the consent form (Google User Messaging Platform).

These SDKs may collect and send to their providers:

  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • Approximate location (derived from your IP address)
  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language, network type)
  • Ad interactions and diagnostics (ads shown, clicks, performance and crash data)

Unity and Google use this data to show, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in the app's settings. Learn more: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads), and manage ad personalisation at My Ad Center.

Purchases

If the app offers an in-app purchase (for example "Remove ads"), it is processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details; Google Play only tells the app whether the purchase is owned.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for families.

Sharing and security

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with our advertising partners (Unity and Google) and with Google Play, as described above, over encrypted connections.

Your rights

You can ask us for information about, or deletion of, any data we may hold by email. Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data should also be sent to Unity or Google.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page will change when we do.
