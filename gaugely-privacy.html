Privacy Policy - Gaugely

Last updated: 2026-11-24

Gaugely: Car Log & Fuel ("the app") is a car logbook app published by Hamri (contact: hamri2985@gmail.com). The app is free and is supported by ads (banner and interstitial ads) provided by Google AdMob and Unity LevelPlay. Ads are being added in an upcoming update; this policy already covers them.

Summary

  • Your vehicle, fuel, cost and reminder data is stored only on your device.
  • The app has no accounts and no server. We do not collect personal data ourselves.
  • The advertising SDKs collect some device and usage data and share it with their providers.
  • We do not sell your data.

Data stored on your device

Saved only on your device: your vehicles, fuel entries, other costs, odometer readings, reminders and your settings. This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you delete the vehicle(s), clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Backups

If Android Auto Backup is enabled on your phone, Android may back up the app's data to your own Google account. This is controlled by you in your device settings and is governed by Google's terms. We cannot access it. Files you export from the app are saved only where you choose.

Camera and photos

The app does not currently use the camera or your photos. If a future version offers scanning (for example of a receipt or odometer), images will be processed on your device and not uploaded by us, and the camera permission will be requested only when you use that feature.

Notifications

The app can show reminder notifications (for example service, inspection or insurance dates). These are created locally on your device; no data is sent to a server for this.

Advertising (Google AdMob and Unity LevelPlay)

The app uses Google AdMob through Unity LevelPlay (which may also serve ads from Unity Ads and other ad networks). The SDKs may collect and send to their providers:

  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • Approximate location (derived from your IP address)
  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language, network type)
  • Ad interactions and diagnostics (ads shown, clicks, performance and crash data)

This data is used to show, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice later in the app under Settings > Privacy options. Learn more: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Unity Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads), and manage ad personalisation at My Ad Center.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for families.

Sharing and security

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with the ad providers described above, over encrypted connections.

Your rights and deletion

We hold no personal data about you on any server. You can delete all app data by deleting your vehicles or uninstalling the app. You can ask us by email about any data we may hold. Requests about advertising data should also be sent to the ad providers.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page will change when we do.
