Privacy Policy - Patchloom

Last updated: 2026-11-23

Patchloom ("the app") is a puzzle game published by Hamri (contact: hamri2985@gmail.com). The app is free and shows ads (banner, interstitial and rewarded) through Unity LevelPlay, with Unity Ads and Google AdMob as ad partners.

Summary

  • Your game data (best score, settings, saved game) stays on your device.
  • The app has no accounts and we do not collect personal data ourselves.
  • The advertising SDKs that show the ads (Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads and Google Mobile Ads) collect some device and usage data and share it with their providers.

Data stored on your device

Saved only on your device (Android SharedPreferences): your best score, your Sound and Haptics settings, and the state of an unfinished game. This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Advertising partners

Ads in the app are delivered by these partners, whose SDKs are built into the app:

  • Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies, including ironSource): ad mediation that chooses which ad network shows each ad.
  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies): an ad network that serves ads through LevelPlay.
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google): an ad partner, and the consent form (Google User Messaging Platform).

These SDKs may collect and send to their providers:

  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • Approximate location (derived from your IP address)
  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language, network type)
  • Ad interactions and diagnostics (ads shown, clicks, performance and crash data)

Purposes: serving and measuring ads, personalizing ads (where you consent or where permitted), analytics, and fraud prevention and security. This data is shared with Unity and Google as ad providers; we do not sell it and do not receive your personal data. The app needs an internet connection and the INTERNET permission for ads, and declares the advertising ID (AD_ID) permission, which comes with the ads SDKs.

How Unity uses this data: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. How Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. See also Google's Privacy Policy.

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform, UMP) on first launch, before ads are requested. You can choose whether ads may be personalized. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy options. Outside these regions, ads may be personalized according to Google's policies and your device settings.

Your advertising ID choices

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings (typically Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads). If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will not be based on your advertising ID. If you reset the ID, a new one is created.

Deletion

We hold no personal data about you. Game data is removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling. Data held by Unity or Google for advertising is governed by their privacy policies (linked above); you can manage Google ad data at My Ad Center and send data requests to Unity through its privacy policy.

Backups

Android may include the app's local data (best score, settings, saved game) in your Google account's device backup if you have backup enabled. This is handled by Android and Google under your own device settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new date.
